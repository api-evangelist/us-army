A VA Disability Claim Denial is a setback, but it is not a defeat. The system is designed to be rigorous, and unfortunately, that means many valid claims are initially rejected. Whether it’s a missing nexus letter, a botched C&P exam, or a simple paperwork error, these obstacles can be overcome with persistence and the right strategy.  At USMilitary.com, we believe every veteran deserves the benefits they were promised when they took the oath. Don't let a "no" from a rater be the final word on your health and your future. Use the tools available, gather your evidence, and keep fighting. For more help identifying common pitfalls, read our guide on Common Reasons for Veterans Disability Claim Denials and take the next step toward the rating you’ve earned.
